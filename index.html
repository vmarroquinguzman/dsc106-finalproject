What Does the Future Hold?

Climate projections suggest that San Diego's summers could become warmer as greenhouse gas emissions continue to influence global temperatures. But how much could temperatures change?

Using NASA climate projections, this interactive visualization allows you to compare possible future summer temperatures across San Diego County under different greenhouse gas emissions scenarios.
