Prototype Writeup

So far, we have created a rough outline of our webpage with several sections and added initial drafts of our main visualizations. We created an interactive globe that rotates as the user scrolls using CMIP6 data to show global warming over time. We also created our first version of the San Diego temperature map using downscaled LOCA2-CMIP6 data, which allows users to compare projected summer temperatures from 2015 to 2100 under different emissions scenarios. We added a draggable comparison slider, temperature tooltips, and a ZIP-code search that highlights the user's selected area. We have also started integrating these visualizations into a larger narrative about climate change and its potential future impacts. We expect the most challenging part of our project to be adding animations and creating a cohesive, interactive, and engaging story throughout the webpage. We plan to guide users through the following sequence: introduce global climate change using historical warming data, transition into San Diego County and its recent heatwaves, prompt users to enter their ZIP code, and explore potential future temperatures under different emissions scenarios. We may also add a comparison of warming in coastal versus inland regions to help users understand how the impacts of warming may vary across the county. One challenge will be making the transitions between sections feel clean and smooth since don't have much experience with this type of animation yet, but we are looking forward to experimenting with this and making our webpage look really cool. We also want to balance providing enough context and scientific background to explain the data with keeping the webpage engaging and easy to navigate.
